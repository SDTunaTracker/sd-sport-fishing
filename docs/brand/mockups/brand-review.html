02 · Logo directions

Three ways to sign the work

All three drop "The" from the mark (the domain can keep it). A and B keep the current name; C shows how the system carries a rename to Railside, the strongest new name from the naming study.

A · Tail & Tally · recommended

The idea: three count bars, rising, become a tuna's crescent tail. Data turning into fish is the product in one shape. The bars read as "counts", the tail reads as "offshore", and the mark still works if coverage grows past tuna. Fraunces 600 matches the site's headings; the DM Mono descriptor carries the scope.

Reversed on pine

Stacked, for merch and the About page

One-color symbol, and the tail-only cut for 16–32 px

B · Logbook seal

The idea: a chart-table seal with Point Loma's coordinates around an italic TT monogram. Feels heritage and premium; good on hats and stickers. Weaker as an app icon (letters, not a shape) and tied to one place.

C · If renamed: Railside

The idea: the boat rail as a baseline with stanchions as tally marks. Species-neutral and built to scale ("Railside Monterey"). Only worth it if you choose to rename; see the naming study.

03 · App icon and favicon

The full Tail & Tally mark for the app icon; the tail alone below 48 px, where the bars would blur. The icon keeps clear of the corner mask so iOS and Android maskable crops both work.

08 · Naming shortlist

Keep the name, or rename before the app ships?

"Tuna Tracker" is narrower than the product (yellowtail, dorado, booking, Co-Captain) but memorable and clear of conflicts. The site is only six months old and the app isn't published, so a rename is as cheap now as it will ever be. Checks below are preliminary web and registry lookups from today, not legal clearance; get an attorney's trademark search before committing to any name.
