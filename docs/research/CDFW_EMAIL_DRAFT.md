Hello,

I run Tunatracker (thetunatracker.com), a free website that summarizes daily public fish counts from California sportfishing landings for anglers choosing an open-party trip.

I'd like to request aggregated Commercial Passenger Fishing Vessel (CPFV) logbook data to give our users long-term context. For example: how this season compares with past years, and which areas have historically produced which species in a given month. The specifics:

- **Content:** fish kept (and released, if available), number of anglers or angler-days, and trip counts.
- **Grouping:** by CDFW fishing block, month and species. Trip length or trip type would also help if available.
- **Ports:** San Diego, Orange County/Los Angeles (including Oceanside, Dana Point, Newport, Long Beach and San Pedro) and the San Francisco Bay Area (including Emeryville, Berkeley, Sausalito and Half Moon Bay).
- **Years:** as far back as available, ideally 1980 to the most recent complete year.
- **Species of most interest:** bluefin tuna, yellowfin tuna, yellowtail and dorado. Also skipjack, albacore and bigeye in Southern California, and rockfish, lingcod, salmon, Dungeness crab, striped bass, halibut and sturgeon in the Bay Area.

We only need data in whatever aggregated form your confidentiality rules allow, such as suppressing cells with fewer than three vessels or combining blocks into districts. We don't need vessel- or operator-level records.

Could you also tell me:

1. Whether this data can be shown publicly on our website, and what attribution or terms of use apply.
2. Whether a formal data request form, a fee or a data-use agreement is needed, and the usual turnaround.
3. Whether a recurring annual update is possible.

If it would help, we're happy to share our cleaned daily count data, or note any discrepancies we find against the published landing counts.
