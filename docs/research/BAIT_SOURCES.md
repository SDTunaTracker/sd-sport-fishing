# Live-bait sources for "Pack for this trip"

*Research date: 2026-11-25. Research only: no accounts, logins, calls or purchases. We fetched about 40 public pages, one at a time, about 2 s apart. We didn't fetch Facebook or Instagram (their robots.txt blocks automated access). Phone numbers and prices are as published on the dates shown; confirm before relying on them. Not legal advice.*

## Summary for the owner

**Good news: San Diego's bait dock publishes exactly what we need.**
- Everingham Bros. Bait Co. runs the live-bait barges in **San Diego Bay, Mission Bay and Dana Point**, the bait source for H&M, Fisherman's, Point Loma, Seaforth and Dana Wharf boats.
- On its own website it publishes a small **"Everingham Bait Report"** table: per barge, the bait species and size range, with an "As of" time.
- Today it read:
  - **San Diego Bay:** "5-7 inch Sardine", as of 11/25 8:45a
  - **Mission Bay:** "4-6 inch Sardine", as of 8:16a
  - **Dana Point:** "3-5 inch Anchovy", as of 8:14a
- It's a published Google Sheet, so it can be read as a tiny CSV with no login. That covers **every San Diego landing plus Dana Point**, updated the same morning.

**Outside San Diego and Dana Point, no bait dock publishes anything machine-readable today.**
- **LA/OC (Newport, San Pedro, Long Beach):** San Pedro Bait Co. runs the Newport and San Pedro barges. It used to post bait reports on the fish-count network (2009–2017; one possibly in Aug 2025), but there's no current feed.
- **Marina del Rey, Redondo, Oxnard/Ventura and the SF Bay:** bait is sold by landings and bait shops that don't post sizes online. SF Bay's J&P Bait posted size reports ("3-6 inch anchovy") until 2024.
- For these ports the feature has to fall back to what boats and anglers say: landing "Update" text, BloodyDecks reports, and later our own users. Or it can show a "call the bait barge" link.

**Recommended plan:**
1. Read Everingham's bait table about every 30 minutes, store each change, and show "Bait on the barge now: 5–7" sardine (as of 8:45 am)" on SD and Dana Point trip cards.
2. Fill gaps with the landing's own "Update" line (Fisherman's said "Sardines are currently on board" today) and BloodyDecks bait mentions.
3. Turn bait + target species + trip length into a packing list using a reference table that **cites the landings' own tackle pages**. H&M and Point Loma publish hook, line and sinker lists. Always end with "confirm with the deckhand".
4. **Email Everingham first** (baitbarge@aol.com, their published business address) to say we'd like to show their bait report with credit and a link. Their site has no usable terms text and robots.txt allows everything, but a quick OK turns a grey area into a partnership. A draft is in §6.

**What it costs:** $0. The Sheet is about 0.7 KB per read.

## 4. Access and terms details

- **Everingham Sheet:**
  - The `pub?…output=csv` URL is Google's "Publish to the web" feature: the owner chose to make it public.
  - Fetch it no more than every 15–30 minutes with a descriptive User-Agent, store only changes, and always show "Source: Everingham Bros. Bait Co." with a link to baitbarge.com/bait-report.
  - Echo their own caveat: "Actual bait offered at the barges may differ".
  - If the sheet id changes, scrape the `docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/…/pubhtml` link from baitbarge.com/bait-report (one request a day).
- **Meta (Facebook/Instagram):** see the hard flag above. A human (owner or staff) may *read* the posts and type a correction into our admin if the sheet is stale. Automated reading is out.
- **TECK network (fishcounts / SFR / socal / norcal / landing sites):**
  - [SFR Terms](https://www.sportfishingreport.com/terms.php) (2026-09-01) §1.2 and §3.1 forbid automated access and building databases without written permission.
  - The owner accepted this for **counts**. Using the landing "Update" narrative for bait is a small extension of the same risk. Name it in the data-licence conversation (THIRD_MARKET §7).
- **976-TUNA:** behind a Cloudflare challenge. Using a harvested `cf_clearance` cookie is a bot-detection workaround. Recommend the owner review `scripts/check_cf_cookie.py` and the OC/LA backfill that depends on it.
