Privacy

Your privacy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Tunatracker shows public fish counts from San Diego's sportfishing landings. You can use almost all of it without an account. This page explains, in plain language, what the site collects, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • We use Google Analytics to see which pages and features people use.
  • If you sign in, your account is handled by Clerk, and your saved preferences are stored with it.
  • Boat reviews you post are public. We store a shortened, one-way hash of your IP address with each review to fight spam.
  • Messages you type into the trip-planning chat are sent to Anthropic's AI to write the reply.
  • Saved boats, map waypoints and display settings are stored in your browser.

When you browse

The site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies and records things like the pages you visit, your general location and device type, and some actions on the site. Examples include which trip you click in the Trip Planner (boat, dates, price) and which filters you apply. We use this to understand what's useful and what to improve. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives your IP address and basic request details when you load a page.

If you create an account

Sign-in is provided by Clerk. Clerk stores the details you sign up with (such as your email address and name) and handles passwords and sign-in. See Clerk's privacy policy.

When you're signed in, preferences such as the boats you follow are saved to your Clerk account profile so they follow you across devices.

Boat reviews

When you post a review, we store what you submit: the boat and trip details, your ratings, the title and text, whether you'd rebook, and the name you enter (or "Anonymous"). Reviews are shown publicly on boat pages.

  • Photos you attach are uploaded to Cloudinary, an image-hosting service, and shown with your review.
  • Spam protection: to limit each visitor to 5 reviews per hour, we keep a counter tied to your IP address for about an hour. We also store a shortened, one-way hash of your IP address with each review. A hash is a scrambled code that can't be turned back into the address, but it lets us spot repeated abuse.

Trip-planning chat

On the Trip Planner, the chat assistant sends your message, the earlier messages in that conversation, and a summary of the page you're on through our relay server to Anthropic, which provides the AI model that writes the answer. Our relay passes the conversation along and doesn't save it. Anthropic handles it under its own policies. Please don't type personal details you don't want shared into the chat.

Stored in your browser

Some features remember things using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device unless noted:

  • Saved or followed boats. When you're signed in, followed boats are also synced to your account.
  • Map waypoints you add on the Charts page
  • Your region, map and display settings
  • Trips you've recently viewed, and a log of the trips you've clicked
  • A daily count of chat messages, used to apply the chat limit

The site also installs a service worker that caches pages so they load quickly and work offline.

Your location

"Locate me" on the Charts page asks your browser for your location only when you tap it, and only if you allow it. It's used in your browser to center the map. The site doesn't send your location to us.

Your choices

  • Clear what's stored on your device by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Limit analytics with your browser's tracking protection, a content blocker, or Google's opt-out add-on.
  • Ask us to delete your account or a review you posted by emailing us (below).

Changes and contact

If we change how the site handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
